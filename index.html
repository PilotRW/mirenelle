What we do

We run sourcing, marketplace sales and fulfilment as one integrated retail business.

Sourcing original products

We buy branded fragrances and cosmetics from manufacturers, authorized distributors and wholesalers, with an invoice for every delivery.

Marketplace sales

We list and sell products on Amazon and eBay, with accurate product information and attentive customer service.

Careful handling

Products are stored and shipped from our logistics center in Germany, protected from heat, light and damage.
